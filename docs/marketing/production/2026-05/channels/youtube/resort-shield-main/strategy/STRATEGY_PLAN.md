# Resort Shield: Strategic Full-Funnel Marketing Plan (May 2026)

This campaign focuses on the reliability and high-traffic performance of
GateFlow in **Sahl Hasheesh & Hurghada** resort environments.

---

## 1. Funnel Map (**FUNNEL_MAP.md**)

### TOFU (Top of Funnel) - Awareness

- **Asset:** "Resort Shield: The Sahl Hasheesh Standard" (16:9 YouTube Flagship).
- **Goal:** Show scale, security infrastructure, and seamless guest arrival.
- **DNA:** High-Production Cinematic 4K.

### MOFU (Middle of Funnel) - Consideration

- **Asset:** "How HMAC Stops Cloning in 2,000+ Room Resorts" (LI Carousel/YT Deep).
- **Goal:** Technical deep-dive for hotel operations managers.
- **DNA:** Professional Studio (5000K Lighting).

### BOFU (Bottom of Funnel) - Conversion

- **Asset:** "Upgrade Your Legacy Resort Access Today" (Meta/LI Lead Ads).
- **Goal:** Direct lead-gen for enterprise demos.
- **DNA:** Performance-Ad Hook with Urgency.
