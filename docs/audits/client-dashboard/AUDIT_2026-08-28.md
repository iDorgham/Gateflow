## 3. Architecture & Functional Modules

### A. Authentication & Multi-Tenant Scoping

- **Auth Layer:** NextAuth.js JWT session with cross-subdomain cookie sharing (`AUTH_COOKIE_DOMAIN=.gateflow.site`) and single-session revocation tokens.
- **Tenant Isolation:** Request-level `requireAuth` helper and `AsyncLocalStorage` ensure all database operations are scoped by `organizationId`.

### B. Access Control & Gate Management

- **QR Lifecycle:** Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signing, replay prevention via nonce tracking, and direct PostgreSQL ID binding.
- **Batch Processing:** `/api/scans/bulk` rate-limited, guarded by `Gate Operator` / `Security Manager` role checks, capped at 500 scans/request, and secured with `skipDuplicates: true`.
- **Guard Shift Attribution:** Shift clock-in/clock-out tracking with time bounds and gate assignment validation.

### C. Autonomous Operations & GateAI

- **AI Assistant:** Vercel AI SDK v6 multi-part UIMessage architecture with explicit tool execution and human-in-the-loop confirmation.
- **Facility Maintenance:** Autonomous fault detection, Kanban dispatching, and vendor QR pass generation.

### D. Analytics & Reporting

- High-density operational data tables with sorting, filtering, CSV export, and PDF summary exports.
- Real-time event streaming via SSE (`/api/events/stream`).
