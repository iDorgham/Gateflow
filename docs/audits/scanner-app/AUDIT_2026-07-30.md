## Executive verdict

Scanner App is a working single-shell Expo operator client with SecureStore
auth, HMAC QR verify, encrypted offline queue (`scanUuid`), and multi-tab
operator UX. It is **not pilot-ready**: biometrics/secure PIN exist but are
unwired into the shell, no shift session gate, no i18n/RTL, docs describe a
non-existent Expo Router tree, and scanner-owned pilot steps lack device
evidence. Ready plan `docs/plan/Ready/scanner_onboarding_session/` remains
Planned (Phase 01 hooks partially present, Phases 02–05 unchecked).
