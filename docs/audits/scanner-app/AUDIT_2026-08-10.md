## Executive verdict

All five phases of `docs/plan/Active/scanner_onboarding_session/` have
shipped code (Phases 01–05, per `TASKS_scanner_onboarding_session.md`):
device unlock (PIN/biometric) gates the shell, `EXPO_PUBLIC_QR_SECRET`
fails closed, shift sessions block scanning when no shift is active, a new
home dashboard replaced the old tab-first layout, and a global
`BiometricGuard` re-locks the shell after 5 minutes idle or on
backgrounding. Three of the prior audit's four P0 items are now closed in
source; the fourth (device-proven pilot evidence) is **still open and
explicitly blocked** — this environment has no physical device and the iOS
Simulator's camera cannot read a real QR code, a constraint the Phase 05
log documents directly rather than fabricating evidence around it. Scanner
App remains **not pilot-certified**: `PILOT_GATE_2026-07-30.json` is
unchanged (both scanner-owned steps still `partial`), and two gaps from the
prior audit — docs describing a non-existent tech stack, and no actual
Arabic/RTL language support (only a _layout_ audit, not i18n) — persist
unresolved.
