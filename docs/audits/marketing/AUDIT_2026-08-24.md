## 1. Overview & Architectural Role

`marketing` is GateFlow's high-conversion public showcase, product documentation gateway, and inbound lead acquisition engine. It communicates GateFlow's zero-trust digital perimeter value proposition to compound developers, enterprise property managers, and security directors.

### Core Architecture

- **Framework:** Next.js 16 App Router with static generation (SSG) and incremental revalidation (ISR).
- **Internationalization:** Full Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR) bilingual site powered by `next-intl`.
- **Styling & Aesthetics:** Antigravity Design System (ADS) tokens, Tailwind v4, glassmorphism, Framer Motion micro-interactions.
- **Lead Capture:** Direct integration with GateFlow Lead CRM and automated qualification bots.

## 3. Pros and Cons Analysis

### Pros (Strengths)

1. **High Visual Impact:** Rich aesthetic with dark mode support, animated product mockups, and interactive feature tours.
2. **Native MENA Localization:** Perfect Arabic typography (Cairo/Tajawal), RTL layout mirroring, and culturally resonant messaging.
3. **Automated Blog Pipeline:** Reads published CMS blog posts generated via `admin-dashboard`.

### Cons (Weaknesses & Tech Debt)

1. **SSRF Wildcard Risk:** Image hostname allowlists in `next.config.js` should restrict wildcards to explicit image CDNs.
2. **TypeScript Strictness:** Transition to strict typing (`"strict": true`) to match core packages.
