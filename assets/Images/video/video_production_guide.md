## 🎬 Master AI Video Prompt

**Prompt for Video AI:**

> "A cinematic 4K video showing a smooth transition between a luxury SUV approaching a high-tech security gate and then driving into a premium residential compound in Hurghada.
>
> **Shot Type:** Wide Establishing Shot transitioning to a Medium Tracking Shot.
> **Camera Movement:** Start with a slow crane down towards the car as it approaches the gate. As the gate opens automatically (signifying GateFlow access), the camera performs a smooth gimbal transition to follow the car from behind (over-the-shoulder tracking).
> **Sequence Flow:** The SUV slows down at the gate, waits for a split second (system verification), the gate lifts smoothly with integrated LED signals turning green, and the car accelerates gently into the compound.
> **Visual Atmosphere:** Mediterranean coastal light, vibrant blooming bougainvillea, crisp palm tree shadows on the pavement, clear turquoise water visible on the horizon. High-end property commercial aesthetic.
> **Transitions:** Use the provided start image for frame 0 and end image for the final frame. Maintain temporal consistency of the white SUV and the architectural style."

## 💡 Pro Tips for Generation

- **Motion Buckets (Runway):** Set motion to 4-5 for realistic vehicle movement.
- **Camera Motion (Luma):** Use `-motion 5` or `follow car` commands.
- **Negative Prompts:** Avoid "glitchy gates," "sudden car color changes," or "unrealistic palm trees."
